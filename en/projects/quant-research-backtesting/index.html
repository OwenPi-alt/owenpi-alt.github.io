CASE 03 / ENGINEERING IN DETAIL

Quant Research & Backtesting

Reconcile signals, costs, and cash movements before interpreting a backtest.

Personal research · AI-assisted · Simulated backtesting prototype

Context and constraints

A backtest depends on signal calculations, execution assumptions, costs, and capital constraints. This personal research prototype examines those layers separately: why a signal was produced, when costs were deducted, how risk rules changed cash and positions, and whether results can be reproduced after data or code changes. Its focus is a research tool whose calculations can be inspected and reconciled.

Design decisions and implementation

01

Separate signal generation from capital accounting

ChallengeAn engine’s final statistics alone make it difficult to identify whether a discrepancy comes from signals, execution assumptions, or capital calculations.

ApproachExported structured strategy signals from LEAN / C# for an independent Python ledger to consume and simulate cash and execution. Separate window calculations support row-by-row reconciliation, while the signal stage places no orders.

ValueProvides an independent inspection point for each layer and a way to locate the source of discrepancies.

02

Use the same capital basis for costs and risk

ChallengeDeducting costs in a report without reflecting them in cash and risk decisions can distort research conclusions.

ApproachDistinguished trading equity, net equity, actual cash deductions, and return attribution. The ledger includes commissions, slippage, funding fees, and settlement, with risk controls based on net equity after costs.

ValueMakes risk rules act on the capital model and enables checks for missing costs, double counting, and incorrect attribution.

03

Keep a reproducible path from inputs to results

ChallengeSummary charts alone cannot explain result changes when inputs, parameters, or code are revised.

ApproachRetained individual trades, decision reasons, cost records, and risk events. Scripts connect signal validation, backtesting, and ledger acceptance checks, with hashes for inputs, code, and outputs.

ValueConnects research reports to their underlying calculations and establishes a reproducible baseline for later experiments.

Engineering value

Provides a basis for explainable, reconcilable, and reproducible research through explicit ledger and cost assumptions.

Validation focus

  • Does row-by-row reconciliation reveal missing signals, alignment errors, or duplicate consumption?
  • Do cost deductions and risk pauses actually change available cash, positions, and subsequent entries?
  • Can the same inputs, code, and parameters reproduce both detailed records and aggregate results?

Research scope

An AI-assisted personal research prototype covering signal calculations, an independent capital ledger, risk rules, result reconciliation, and HTML research reports.

Current stage

Research and simulated backtesting only. The prototype is not connected to a brokerage account, does not place live orders, and is not a client production system.
