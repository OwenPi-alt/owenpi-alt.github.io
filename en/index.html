01 / BUSINESS SYSTEMS

A request arrives. How do we establish what happened?

A design walkthrough using order notifications and customer messaging to examine intake, rules, asynchronous work, and device acknowledgments.

DESIGN DECISION / 01

What does a successful response actually confirm?

Request acceptance, business completion, and device acknowledgment need separate records. Define what the response means and carry a business identifier into later tasks. Otherwise, a caller retrying after a timeout cannot easily tell whether the original request ran.

  • Does a successful response mean acceptance or completion?
  • How are duplicates identified and status checked?
  • Can a request be traced through the work that follows?
